# `prices.csv` sources

Download date: **2026-10-22**.

`prices.csv` contains monthly adjusted-close prices for the 60 monthly observations from November 2021 through October 2026, in oldest-to-newest order. The final October 2026 observation is the latest available price on the download date.

| CSV column | Proxy | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BIL` | SPDR Bloomberg 1-3 Month T-Bill ETF | [Yahoo Finance monthly adjusted-close data](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/BIL?interval=1mo) |
| `IEF` | iShares 7-10 Year Treasury Bond ETF | [Yahoo Finance monthly adjusted-close data](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/IEF?interval=1mo) |
| `LQD` | iShares iBoxx $ Investment Grade Corporate Bond ETF | [Yahoo Finance monthly adjusted-close data](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/LQD?interval=1mo) |
| `ACWI` | iShares MSCI ACWI ETF | [Yahoo Finance monthly adjusted-close data](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/ACWI?interval=1mo) |

Adjusted-close prices incorporate the provider's adjustments for distributions and splits. Any return calculated from these five years is a historical annualized estimate, not a forecast.

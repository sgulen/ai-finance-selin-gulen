# S1–S10 classification

- S1 — **hard constraint**: It specifies a seven-year horizon and an annual rebalancing cadence.
- S2 — **objective**: It states the desired CPI + 4% net-of-costs return over rolling five-year periods.
- S3 — **hard constraint**: It requires one year of distributions to be held in cash or daily-liquid government securities.
- S4 — **preference**: The benchmark is a comparison reference, not a stated portfolio-limit rule.
- S5 — **hard constraint**: It prohibits borrowing, short sales, and derivatives.
- S6 — **hard constraint**: It caps exposure to any one issuer at 8%.
- S7 — **hard constraint**: It caps annual turnover at 25% unless committee approval is obtained.
- S8 — **hard constraint**: It requires at least 90% of the reserve to be redeemable within five business days.
- S9 — **unverifiable promise**: It gives neither a defined loss measure nor a breach rule for the 10% limit.
- S10 — **missing**: No sector, ESG, tax, or home-bias preference—or pilot optimization target—has been specified.
